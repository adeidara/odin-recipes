Tortilla Pinwheel Kebabs with Yogurt Sauce

Tortilla pinwheel kabobs with yogurt sauce offer a whole new way to present ordinary ground beef. Seasoned with garlic and spices, baked in a very hot oven, and served with a lemony dipping sauce, these skewers are extra delicious (and fun).

Ingredients

  • 1 to 1 1/4 pounds ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, grated
  • 2 cloves garlic, pressed or grated
  • 2 teaspoons paprika
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt, divided
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • 6 (8 inch) flour tortillas
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • wooden skewers, soaked in water for 30 minutes

Directions

step 1

Preheat the oven to 475 degrees F (245 degrees C). Combine ground beef, onion, garlic, paprika, cumin, coriander, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon pepper in a bowl. Use a microplane to grate about 1 teaspoon lemon zest into a small bowl and set aside. Cut lemon in half and squeeze 1 tablespoon of juice into the meat mixture and mix well.

step 2

Spread meat mixture evenly onto the tortillas in a thin layer, leaving a 1/4-inch border around the edges. Roll each tortilla tightly and trim off the ends to form a straight edge. Cut each roll into 3/4-inch slices.
